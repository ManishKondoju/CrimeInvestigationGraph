# brag-plan — CrimeGraphRAG (v2, cinematic)

**Tone:** `cinematic` — trailer-scale, big type, dramatic wipes
**Format:** 1920×1080 @ 30fps, letterboxed to 2.39:1 · **24s (720 frames)**

## What changed from v1

v1 was atmospheric but failed the skill's "clear to a stranger" test: it
showed a query resolving without ever stating what the thing *is*. v2 is
built on a narrative spine — a problem, a turn, a payoff — and each beat
says one plain thing out loud.

## The spine

> 670 crimes sit in a database. The links between them are invisible.
> Make it a graph, and you can just *ask*. And it shows you its working.

## Cinematic grammar

- **2.39:1 letterbox** — bars present from frame 0
- **Camera** — continuous slow push-in per scene; never a static frame
- **Parallax** — grid and map drift at different rates from the type
- **Wipes** — a hazard bar sweeps the cut between acts, not a crossfade
- **Masked type** — lines reveal under a moving mask rather than fading
- **Impact** — a one-frame white flash on the title and the payoff
- **Grain + vignette** — pulsing, heavier in the dark beats

## Readability (0.3s/word, after settle)

Longest line is scene 2 at 6 words → 1.8s needed, 2.67s given. Scene 6's
answer runs 12 words → 3.6s needed, 5.0s given.

## Sound

Trailer score: sub drop on the cold open, a rising drone through the
problem, a riser into the title with an impact, rhythmic pulse under the
query, and a full chord on the outro. Effects written into the same key
(D minor) and mixed under the music.
